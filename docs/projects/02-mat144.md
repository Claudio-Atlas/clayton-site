# MAT144.com: a course companion site for college math

**One-line summary:** A mobile-friendly study site that gives students in a college math course walkthroughs, interactive practice and exam reviews alongside their regular course materials.

## Overview

Students in a large introductory math course had a learning platform and an adaptive homework system, but few places to see worked steps, practice interactively, or find out what an exam would actually cover. I built a companion site for the course's online and on-ground sections. It covers seven topics with mini lessons, step-by-step review questions, discussion-assignment walkthroughs, interactive tools and a video library.

## What I did

- Built 74 step-by-step review questions with 271 practice problems, plus exam-review pages that map each exam question to practice.
- Organized a video library of 174 entries, 144 of them published.
- Redesigned the phone experience. The homepage went from 6 screens of scrolling to 2, the video library from 71 to about 5, and assignment pages from about 18 to 8.
- Wrote automated checks. One loads every page at phone, tablet and laptop sizes in a headless browser and flags layout breakage before deployment.
- Refactored a 50,000-line file into a 2,100-line reusable engine plus a separate course content package. I verified the refactor by comparing all 292 pages byte for byte.
- Designed the site with no accounts and no student data: progress is stored only on the student's own device.
