## Overview

Students in a large introductory math course had a learning platform and an adaptive homework system, but few places to see worked steps, practice interactively, or find out what an exam would actually cover. I built a companion site for the course's online and on-ground sections. It covers seven topics with mini lessons, step-by-step review questions, discussion-assignment walkthroughs, interactive tools and a video library.

## What I built

- **74 step-by-step review questions** with 271 practice problems, plus exam-review pages that map each exam question to practice.
- **A video library** of 174 entries, 144 of them published, all produced with my Orbital pipeline.
- **A redesigned phone experience.** The homepage went from 6 screens of scrolling to 2, the video library from 71 to about 5, and assignment pages from about 18 to 8.
- **Automated layout checks.** Every page is loaded at phone, tablet and laptop sizes in a headless browser, and layout breakage is flagged before deployment.
- **A clean architecture.** I refactored a 50,000-line file into a 2,100-line reusable engine plus a separate course content package, and verified the refactor by comparing all 292 pages byte for byte.
- **Private by design.** No accounts and no student data: progress is stored only on the student's own device.
